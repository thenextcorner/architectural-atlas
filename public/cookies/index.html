Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Architectural Atlas uses as few as possible, and statistics cookies only load after you accept them in the cookie banner.

Changing your choice

Use the Cookie settings link in the footer of the home page to reopen the banner and change your choice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site works fully without them.
