What the site is

An interactive, educational visualization of famous structures. The 3D models are schematic illustrations generated procedurally from public reference material. They are simplified, not to scale in every detail, and are not engineering documents. Never use them for construction, renovation, navigation, or safety decisions.

Accuracy

Component names and facts are researched from public sources, listed in the site's attribution. Errors are possible in a hobby project; if you spot one, report it via dennis@thenextcorner.com and it will be corrected.
