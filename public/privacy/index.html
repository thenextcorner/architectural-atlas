Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Architectural Atlas is a hobby project by Dennis Goedegebuure. This policy explains what data the site handles. Contact: .

What we collect directly

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no contact forms, no comments, and no newsletter. The 3D models and pages are served as static files. We do not ask for or store your name, email address, or any other personal details.

Analytics

We measure visits with Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager. Analytics only runs if you accept statistics cookies in the cookie banner. When active, Google collects the pages you view, approximate location, device type, and browser, and processes it under Google's own privacy terms. If you reject cookies, no analytics code loads at all.

Cookies

The site sets one first-party cookie, atlas_consent, which remembers your cookie choice for 12 months. Google Analytics sets its own cookies only after you accept. See the cookie policy for details.

Bot protection

The voting leaderboard uses Cloudflare Turnstile to keep bots from stuffing the ballot. When you vote, Turnstile runs an invisible check in your browser; Cloudflare's handling of the data involved is described in the Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

When a vote is cast, the site stores the vote itself plus a one-way hashed code derived from the voter's IP address. The code exists only so that a second vote for the same structure from the same connection can be refused; the IP address itself is never stored, and the code cannot be turned back into it. The marker expires after 12 months.

Your rights
